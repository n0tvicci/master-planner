## Overview

Build a fully automated faceless YouTube Shorts channel in the military and historical weapons niche. ## 1. The Confirmed Content Formula

Every single video across all 9 analyzed follows this exact structure without exception.

### Script Structure

| Section       | Duration  | Word Count  | Purpose                                                           |
| ------------- | --------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hook          | 5–6 sec   | 15–20 words | Question + familiar object that creates immediate paradox         |
| Misconception | 9–11 sec  | 25–35 words | Validate why the wrong assumption feels completely reasonable     |
| Truth         | 22–34 sec | 70–95 words | Historical or engineering real answer with specific facts         |
| Final Twist   | 10–17 sec | 35–55 words | Ironic consequence showing how the solution created a new problem |

### The Hook Formula

```
"Why did/do [group] [surprising behavior] with [familiar object]?"
```

The hook object must be immediately recognizable to a non-military American on sight. If the object requires explanation before the paradox lands, the topic is too niche.

### The Final Twist Rule

The twist must complete this template:

> "But the final twist is that [the solution] actually [created this new ironic problem]..."

Never end on an additional fact. Always end on an ironic consequence or failure of the main answer. This is what drives comments, replays, and shares.

## 2. Topic Selection System

**This is the most important section.** Analysis of 9 videos confirmed that topic selection — not production quality — is the primary driver of viral performance.

### Topic Tier System

#### Tier 1 — Highest Viral Potential ✅

**Characteristics:**

- Hook object is recognizable to any American, not just military enthusiasts
- Paradox is immediately visual and emotional, not logical
- Answer surprises even people who already know military history
- Final twist feels personally relatable

**Confirmed Tier 1 examples from analysis:**

- MRE Spoon — everyone knows a plastic spoon
- Tank cartoon paint — everyone knows camouflage looks wrong in bright colors
- Lazy Dog bomb — tiny object causing massive destruction is visually immediate

**New Tier 1 topic ideas:**

- Why do soldiers fold their socks a specific way?
- Why did the military paint planes pink?
- Why does military bread last 3 years?
- Why do bulletproof vests have a pocket that looks completely useless?
- Why do soldiers sleep with their boots on?
- Why did the military issue soldiers chewing gum?
- Why do military dogs wear boots?
- Why did WW2 soldiers carry playing cards?

#### Tier 2 — Solid Performers 🟡

**Characteristics:**

- Requires slight military knowledge to appreciate
- Strong engineering or physics paradox
- Clear visual demonstration possible
- US military focus preferred

**Confirmed Tier 2 examples from analysis:**

- Recoilless rifle ear damage
- Grease Gun disposable design
- Flip tank hydrodynamics

#### Tier 3 — Avoid ❌

**Characteristics:**

- Requires significant prior knowledge
- Abstract rather than visual paradox
- Too similar to a recently published video
- Geopolitical comparisons or politically sensitive angles
- Two videos of the same equipment type within 14 days

**Confirmed Tier 3 examples from analysis:**

- USA vs China weapons comparison — geopolitical, politically risky
- Blind tank — too similar to flip tank posted same period

### Topic Tier Scoring Rubric

Score every topic before writing the script:

| Criterion                                                  | Score |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| Hook object recognizable to non-military American on sight | 0–3   |
| Paradox is visual/emotional rather than purely logical     | 0–3   |
| Stakes feel personally relatable to general audience       | 0–2   |
| Strong US military connection                              | 0–2   |

**Minimum scores:**

- Tier 1: 8–10 → pursue immediately
- Tier 2: 6–7 → acceptable, produce after Tier 1 backlog
- Tier 3: Below 6 → reject, generate new topic

### Topic Diversity Rules

- No two scripts in the same 14-day window can cover the same equipment type
- No tank videos within 14 days of each other — confirmed performance drag
- No geopolitical country comparison videos ever
- Maintain a rolling 30-day topic log to enforce diversity

## 4. Content Sourcing & Footage Strategy

### The Confirmed Footage Mix (from 9 videos)

Every ArmorXpress video uses this exact rotation:

| Footage Type                      | Role                                           | Source                                      |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Self-filmed desk prop close-up    | Opens AND closes video — creates seamless loop | Film yourself                               |
| Archival/historical stock footage | Context and era setting                        | DVIDS, National Archives, Wikimedia Commons |
| Custom 3D animations              | Technical truth visualization                  | Runway ML, After Effects                    |
| 3D blueprint/vector diagrams      | Physics and engineering explanation            | After Effects shape layers                  |
| Stock B-roll                      | Narrative support                              | Pexels, Pixabay                             |
| 3D render for final twist         | Visualizes the ironic consequence              | Runway ML                                   |

### The Desk Prop Library — Critical Investment

Self-filmed prop close-ups appear in every single video and cannot be sourced from stock sites. This is a one-time investment that feeds dozens of videos.

**Recommended prop library (~$200–300 total):**

- Inert grenade replica ($20–30)
- Military MRE pack with utensils ($10–15)
- Scale model tanks x2 ($30–60 each)
- Scale model aircraft ($25–40)
- Rifle cleaning kit ($15–20)
- Military gear: dog tags, compass, canteen ($20–30)
- Historical weapon replicas: bayonet, knife ($20–40)
- FP-45 Liberator replica or similar small arms ($30–50)

**Filming instructions:**

- Film 10–15 macro clips of each prop from different angles
- Use a dark desk mat or black surface
- Use a ring light for clean, consistent lighting
- Film on smartphone in 4K
- Always film an opening AND closing shot at matching angles for the loop
- Store all clips in `/footage/prop-library/[prop-name]/`

### The Seamless Loop Rule

**This is mandatory.** Confirmed across all 9 videos without exception.

- The opening clip and closing clip must be the same prop from the same angle
- The final frame must match the starting frame exactly
- This causes the video to replay without the viewer noticing — artificially inflating watch time
- In CapCut: the last clip in your timeline must visually match the first clip

### Footage Clearance Rules

- Strip all background audio from every downloaded clip before assembly
- No copyrighted news broadcast clips ever
- No graphic casualties, gore, or injury animations ever (confirmed monetization risk from Video 1)
- Log source URL, license type, and clearance date for every clip used

## 8. Video Assembly (CapCut)

### Overview

Editing is intentionally simple. The script, voiceover, and footage do all the work. Target: 15 minutes per video in CapCut once all assets are ready.

### Asset Checklist Before Opening CapCut

- [ ] Opening desk prop clip ready (matches closing clip angle)
- [ ] All footage clips downloaded and background audio stripped
- [ ] Voiceover MP3 from ElevenLabs ready
- [ ] Background music track ready (cinematic suspense genre)
- [ ] SFX files ready: bass drop, whooshes, thematic object sound
- [ ] Script open with color pop keywords identified
- [ ] Overlay timing cues from pipeline output

### CapCut Assembly Steps

| Step | Action                                                           | Time  |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| 1    | New project, canvas 9:16 (1080x1920)                             | 1 min |
| 2    | Arrange 24 clips in order, hard cuts only, no transitions        | 5 min |
| 3    | Drop voiceover MP3, align to start                               | 1 min |
| 4    | Drop background music, set to -16 to -20dB, fade last 3 sec      | 1 min |
| 5    | Add SFX: bass drop at hook, whooshes on cuts, thematic sound     | 2 min |
| 6    | Add keyword overlays — ALL CAPS, white, black outline, center    | 3 min |
| 7    | Apply color pop: change key words to neon yellow or red          | 1 min |
| 8    | Auto Caption from voiceover, syllable-by-syllable, center screen | 2 min |
| 9    | Music EQ: dip mid-range at Technical Truth section start         | 1 min |
| 10   | Export: 1080x1920, MP4, 30fps, High Quality                      | 1 min |

**Total: ~18 minutes per video**

### Music EQ Rule (confirmed across all 9 videos)

- At start of Technical Truth section: reduce music mid-range or lower to -22dB
- At start of Final Twist: restore music to -16dB for impact
- This makes the technical explanation clear and the twist land harder

### Caption Style Specification

- Font: Heavy bold sans-serif
- Color: White fill, thick black outline stroke
- NO background box or pill — outline only
- Position: Dead center vertically and horizontally
- Pacing: Syllable-by-syllable — not word-by-word, not sentence-by-sentence

### Keyword Overlay Specification

- 1–3 words maximum per overlay
- ALL CAPS always
- White fill, black outline (matches caption style)
- Key emotional/technical words: neon yellow or crimson red
- Position: Dead center screen
- Confirmed color pop words from analysis: TERRIFIED, CHEAP, BLIND, FATAL, DESTROY, DEAFENING, BILLIONS, CATCH, FAIL

### Pre-Export Quality Checklist

- [ ] Total clip count: 22–25
- [ ] No single clip longer than 4 seconds
- [ ] First frame and last frame match (loop confirmed)
- [ ] Voiceover clearly audible above music at all times
- [ ] Keyword text synced with spoken word
- [ ] Captions accurate and syllable-synced
- [ ] Total length 54–60 seconds
- [ ] No copyrighted audio audible in any clip
